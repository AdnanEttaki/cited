**FAQ:**

Q: Is this just running ChatGPT a few times?
A: No. Value is in the question design (mapped to real buyer intent in your category), cross-engine measurement, source forensics (why the engine picked them, not you), and a fix list ordered by expected impact.

Q: Do results vary between runs?
A: Yes — AI answers are probabilistic and localized. That's why I measure across engines and question types, and report per-run results honestly with timestamps, not a single flattering screenshot.

Q: What do you need from me?
A: Your URL, your top 3 competitors, and (optionally) the queries you wish you appeared for.

Q: Will this improve my Google SEO?
A: It complements it. Some fixes (source coverage, third-party reviews, structured FAQs) help both; the audit targets AI answers specifically.
