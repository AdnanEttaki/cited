01 — BRAND CORE

Who we are

Every decision filters through one feeling: the warm glow of being thought of. We sell the emotion first and the object second.

Mission

Make gifting effortless & meaningful

Curated, giftable, wrapped and delivered on time — so customers look thoughtful without the stress.

Audience

The "Christmas Planner"

Women 28–55 buying for family, partner and kids. Time-poor, emotionally driven, fear of late delivery.

Personality

Warm · Nostalgic · Assured

Feels like an old-world Christmas market, sounds like a friend who already has everything handled.

Promise

Here by Christmas, or it's free

Our core risk-reversal. It turns the #1 holiday fear (lateness) into our #1 reason to buy.

07 — BUYING PSYCHOLOGY

The 10 conversion triggers built into the store

These are the ethical persuasion rules every template uses. Keep them truthful — real stock, real deadlines, real reviews — or they backfire.

1

Urgency

Real shipping cut-off countdowns. Christmas has a hard deadline — show it.

Announcement bar, PDP, cart
2

Scarcity

Low-stock bars and "limited 2026 edition" make waiting feel risky.

PDP stock meter, badges
3

Social proof

Star counts, "sold this week", photo reviews. People follow people.

Hero, cards, PDP, reviews
4

Price anchoring

Show compare-at price first so the real price feels like a win.

Every price display
5

Decoy bundles

1 / 2 / 3-pack where the middle option is the obvious best deal.

PDP bundle selector
6

Goal gradient

"You're $16 away from free shipping" progress bar raises order value.

Cart drawer
7

Risk reversal

"Here by Christmas or it's free" + easy returns removes final hesitation.

Under Add to Cart
8

Reciprocity

Free gift wrap & handwritten note — a gift to the gift-giver.

PDP, cart, emails
9

Choice simplicity

"Shop by who you're buying for" stops decision paralysis.

Homepage, menu
10

Emotion & nostalgia

Sell the moment ("their face when they open it"), not the specs.

Headlines, product copy
08 — VOICE

How we sound

We say

"The ornament they'll hang every year."
"Wrapped, ready, and under the tree by the 24th."
"You've got Christmas handled."
"Loved by 12,000+ families."

We avoid

"BUY NOW!!! HUGE SALE!!!"
"High-quality premium product."
Fake timers that reset on refresh.
Jargon, guilt-tripping, or pushy pop-ups.
