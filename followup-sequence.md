## Per-brand touch-2 hooks (from the live snapshots)

- **Sundays for Dogs** — ChatGPT and Perplexity give opposite verdicts on
  Sundays vs Farmer's Dog. ChatGPT cites your own elevio.help support docs
  against you; Perplexity calls Sundays the value winner. You're running two
  different reputations and probably only measuring one.
- **Bearaby** — You win the generic "best weighted blanket" query on ChatGPT
  and lose the identical query to Baloo on Perplexity. Meanwhile the
  anxiety-intent queries — your brand's whole origin story — name neither of
  you.
- **Loftie** — The "how do I stop doomscrolling at night" query — the exact
  problem the Lamp exists to solve — gets answered with a CHF 7.95 IKEA
  alarm clock. You win "replace my phone" but the problem-aware buyer never
  sees you.
- **Arrae** — The reputation query is brutal: ChatGPT answers "marketing
  stronger than evidence" and pulls the 1,080mg proprietary blend detail
  from the NIH supplement database. That's a citable-facts problem, and it's
  fixable on your side.
- **Bells of Steel** — In the 9-brand "best value garage gym" answer you're
  absent entirely, and Perplexity concedes the *value* argument to REP —
  your home turf. Value positioning only works if the engines repeat it.
- **KraveBeauty** — La Roche-Posay appears in all 5 answers we tested. The
  engines answer skincare from a medical-authority layer (AAD, PubMed,
  newspaper lab tests). Even your barrier-repair query — Great Barrier
  Relief's reason to exist — routes there.
- **Beam** — ChatGPT's answer on your category cites your ClinicalTrials.gov
  record and notes it has no posted results, then calls the category
  "overpriced." A public trial record with no results is a citation asset
  working against you.
- **Native Pet** — The vet-authority layer (VCA, Cornell) owns every answer,
  and the dog-diarrhea query doesn't even mention pumpkin. The engines
  don't know the category exists as a product category.
- **Blueland** — You're the strongest brand we've measured (4/5) — but the
  generic "eco cleaning" query still goes to Seventh Generation and Method,
  and the "non-toxic" frame goes to Branch Basics. Category leader,
  frames owned by others.
- **Great Jones** — Your identity query, "design-forward kitchenware,"
  returns Alessi, HAY, Ferm Living, and Caraway — not you. The engines file
  you under "Dutch oven alternative," which is the smallest shelf you sit on.
- **Apothékary** — On your head-to-head, ChatGPT sides with Moon Juice
  because they publish standardized-exact specs (10% withanolides) and the
  engine reads that as rigor. Meanwhile the skeptic answer on tinctures
  quotes "adaptogenic… balances cortisol" as the textbook marketing red
  flag. The category's frame is being written without you in it.

## Rules

- One thread per prospect, three touches total, then stop. No nagging.
- Never invent a finding for a hook — every hook above is from the real
  snapshot. If a brand replies with a correction, accept it and re-run.
- Track status per prospect in the tracker (who/when/which touch/reply).
