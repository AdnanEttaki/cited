## The pattern

For symptom queries, AI engines bypass brands entirely and cite medical authorities (ACG, NCCIH, PubMed). Where Arrae *is* cited, the sources used against it are its own label (NIH DSLD) and its own marketing page. The leverage points a full audit would map in detail: publish the clinical study with methodology a model can quote (n, design, independent lab), make per-ingredient dosages citable instead of proprietary, and build the third-party evidence layer (dietitian reviews, PubMed-indexed references) that symptom-answers draw from.
