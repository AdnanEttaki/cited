# AI Visibility Snapshot — Sundays for Dogs (sample teaser)

**Engine:** ChatGPT (anonymous, fresh session per question) · **Date:** 2026-08-26
**Method:** 5 real buyer questions for the premium dog-food category, run once each. This is the free 5-question teaser; the full audit runs 50 questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews with multiple samples per question.

## Score: Sundays cited in 2/5 answers — wins 0/5

### Q1. "What's the healthiest dog food brand? My vet told me to upgrade from basic kibble."
**Sundays: NOT cited.** Answer leads with Hill's Science Plan, Purina Pro Plan, Royal Canin, grounded in WSAVA nutrition guidelines. A shopping carousel attached product cards for all three — paid-intent placement Sundays isn't in.

### Q2. "Is fresh dog food actually better than kibble? Which fresh dog food brands are worth it?"
**Sundays: NOT cited.** JustFoodForDogs is the named first choice (veterinary-nutrition involvement, feeding-trial documentation). Category framing — "fresh" — excludes air-dried entirely, so Sundays doesn't even enter the consideration set for a huge query class.

### Q3. "Sundays for Dogs vs The Farmer's Dog — which one is actually better?"
**Sundays: cited — and loses its own comparison.** ChatGPT's verdict: edge to **The Farmer's Dog** ("my winner"), because TFD has board-certified veterinary nutritionists, AAFCO feeding trials and a Cornell study — while noting Sundays "has not yet published peer-reviewed studies." Worse: the sources cited are **Sundays' own help center** (sundays-for-dogs.elevio.help) and the **2026 NAD review** that told Sundays to modify or discontinue several claims. Sundays' own documentation is being used as the evidence against it.

### Q4. "What are the best air-dried dog food brands right now?"
**Sundays: cited, #3 of 5** — behind ZIWI Peak ("best overall") and Open Farm ("best balance"), framed narrowly as "best for picky eaters." This is the one query class Sundays fully owns, and it still doesn't lead it.

### Q5. "My dog has a sensitive stomach. What dog food do vets actually recommend?"
**Sundays: NOT cited.** Hill's i/d, Purina EN, Royal Canin Gastrointestinal, hydrolyzed-protein diets (Hill's z/d, Purina HA). Vet-framed queries route entirely to therapeutic brands.

## What this shows (the fixable part)

1. **The evidence gap is structural, not content-volume.** Farmer's Dog wins Q3 on *published validation* (feeding trials, Cornell study). Sundays' fix: publish nutritionist credentials, digestibility data, AAFCO documentation in crawlable, citation-friendly form.
2. **Your own help center is the citation source — and it's on a third-party subdomain** (elevio.help) with unstructured Q&A. Moving that content into schema-marked pages on sundaysfordogs.com converts it from ammunition-against-you into evidence-for-you.
3. **The NAD review is part of your AI reputation.** ChatGPT cites it verbatim. A public, well-structured response/compliance page changes what the models learn.
4. **Location personalization is real.** Answers localized to Switzerland (CHF prices, Swiss retailers) — citation share must be measured per market, not once.
5. **Shopping carousels are forming.** ChatGPT attached product cards on Q1. Product-feed and schema readiness decides who appears there.
