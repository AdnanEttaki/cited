FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this just SEO with a new label?

No. Classic SEO optimizes for ranked links; AI engines cite sources inside generated answers. The inputs overlap — structure, authority, clarity — but the measurement is completely different. The core of this audit is the citation baseline: direct evidence of what AI engines actually say when buyers ask about your category. No rankings talk, no keyword-stuffing advice.

How do you measure AI citations if answers change?

By sampling honestly. We run each question multiple times per engine, record citation rates rather than single answers, and report the spread. AI answers are probabilistic — anyone promising "guaranteed citations" is lying. What we can show you precisely is where you stand today and which specific gaps the evidence points to.

What if the audit finds nothing wrong?

Then you get documented proof that AI engines already cite you for your category's buying questions — which is worth knowing before you spend on anything else. It's never happened yet, but if it does, we'll say so plainly.
