## 1. The comment → DM automation (this is where the leads come from)
The video asks people to comment **SETTER**. If nobody answers those comments within minutes, the leads go cold. Set up a comment-keyword automation (ManyChat or Instagram's built-in auto-reply) **before** posting:

- **Trigger:** comment contains `SETTER` (also catch `setter`, `Setter`, `SETTER!`)
- **Public reply (rotate 3):** "Sent you a DM 📩" / "Check your DMs 👀" / "Just sent it over 🔵"
- **DM 1:** "Hey {first_name}! Here's the deal: we set up an AI setter that replies to your DMs in seconds, handles the price question, and books calls straight into your calendar (like the one in the video). Quick q: roughly how many DMs/leads do you get a week?"
- **DM 2 (after reply):** "Got it. Most coaches at that volume lose 30–50% of leads to slow replies. Want me to show you how it'd work for your offer? Grab a 15-min slot here: {your booking link}"
- **No reply in 24h:** "Still want to see it running on your DMs? Just reply YES and I'll send a quick demo."

Use your own AI setter for this flow. A live demo of the product is the best proof you can give.

## 4. Posting plan
- Post the 9:16 to Reels + TikTok + Shorts the same day, at your audience's peak hour.
- Pin a comment: "Comment SETTER to see it on your account 👇"
- Reply to the first 20 comments personally within the first hour (the algorithm rewards early engagement).
- Post the 1:1 to the feed/LinkedIn 2–3 days later with a text-led caption.
- Volume wins: plan 1 of these per week with new chat recordings (objection handling, follow-up, no-show recovery). Reuse the pipeline: `.claude/skills/lux-edit`.

## Honest note
No single video guarantees a lead count. Results depend on posting consistency, your offer, and how fast the SETTER comments are answered. Track: views → comments → DMs → booked calls, and improve the weakest step first.
