# Style guide (from the pendulum reference, ref.mp4)

## Rhythm
3 shots in 21 s (cuts.json): the film is one continuous scene with two real cuts (the dark wipe at ~5.5 s, a cream-to-cream swap at ~9.3 s). A 60 BPM pulse: a swish lands on every beat (0.04, 1.04, 2.04 ...), with soft ticks ~0.3 s and ~0.8 s after each. Holds of 0.8-1.5 s let each statement breathe.

## Motion vocabulary
- Entrances: a text line is revealed by a wipe that follows the ball as it passes (left to right); it is erased by the next pass (right to left): on / off / on.
- Exits: blur-fade (about 0.4 s) for the big statement, wipe for the lines.
- Easing: pendulum is a true sine swing (about 2 s period, 1 s per pass). Everything else eases out hard (expo) and settles with a small overshoot (SPRING.snappy / gentle).
- Camera: locked. All motion belongs to the ball.
- Signature move: ONE glowing ball is the thread of the whole film: pendulum -> lamp -> step marker -> logo.

## Composition
Centred type at about 47 % of frame height, tight 2-line stacks. Big negative space. Handle in the top-left corner. The ball keeps a continuous path between scenes, so the eye never has to find the next thing.

## Colour + texture
Cream field (#EBE4D2) with a soft radial vignette, near-black ink (#16171C), one vermilion accent (#E5471E). A dark panel (#14151A) is used once, for the spotlight. Fine grain on top.

## Type in motion
Display face (Inter, 900, tracking -4 %) for statements, uppercase; one italic serif (Playfair) used only for the key word ("booking.", "works", "Setter"). Words appear by wipe, not by fade.

## Do / don't
Do: keep the ball visible in every shot; let each statement hold for at least 1 s; sync every hit to a beat.
Do: use the italic serif for exactly one word per statement.
Do: keep the dark section short and punchy.
Don't: add glows to text or UI chrome, only to the ball.
Don't: invent product screens: the steps use type and simple shapes only.
Don't: fade everything in; use wipes and springs.
