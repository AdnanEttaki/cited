## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium close-up 100%; subject is the in-focus region (no face; shot size is an estimate)
- Camera: fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 72%, moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 28%
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from below (screen / desk bounce / low source); lighting ratio about 1.3:1 (0.3 stops); hard light (sharp shadow edge); colour temperature cool (daylight / cool-white LED); lower half of the subject is brighter: likely light from below (screen, desk bounce); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- (not live footage: the above describes the light in the picture, for generation prompts, not a shooting plan)
- Grade: dark and desaturated (cinematic); low saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 20.1 cuts per minute, 2.62s average shot; no live-action shots
